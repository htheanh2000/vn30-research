- FOMC meeting dates
- COVID wave dates VN (03/2020, 07-09/2021)
- Russia-Ukraine start: 24/02/2022
- VN30F expiry dates: 3rd Thursday each month
- FLC/Trinh Van Quyet arrest: 29/03/2022
