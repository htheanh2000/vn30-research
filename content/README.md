## Research Questions
1. How large is VN30F mispricing vs. cost-of-carry?
2. Do arbitrage bounds get violated? Upper vs. lower?
3. Is there expiry-day manipulation evidence?
4. Do macro shocks & geopolitical panic drive mispricing?
